Delivery & Pickup

We want your Marownie treats to reach you fresh and with care. Choose the pickup or delivery option that works best for you.

Free Pickup

Prefer to pick up your order? Pickup is always free!

Pickup details, including the date, time, and location, will be confirmed personally after we review your order.

Local Delivery

Local delivery is available for an additional fee.

Delivery fees depend on your location and neighborhood. The cost may vary depending on the delivery distance.

Please provide your delivery address when placing your order so we can check availability and calculate the delivery fee.

How Delivery Works

1. Place your dessert order and provide your address.

2. We check the delivery location and calculate the fee.

3. We contact you to confirm the total price and delivery details.

4. Your order is confirmed only after you receive our confirmation.

Have Questions?

Not sure if we deliver to your neighborhood? Contact us with your location, and we'll be happy to help.
