Food Safety & Allergen Information

At Marownie, we prepare our desserts in a home kitchen using carefully selected ingredients. Your safety and satisfaction are important to us.

Allergen Information

Allergen information varies by product, flavor, recipe and purchased ingredients.

Brownies – Main Allergens

Based on the ingredients currently identified for our brownie recipes, the following allergens are present:

  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Wheat
  • Soy (soy lecithin in chocolate)

Additional Allergens by Brownie Flavor

  • Caramel & Peanut Brownie: Peanuts.
  • Cranberry & Peanut Brownie: Peanuts.
  • Pecan Chocolate Brownie: Pecans (Tree Nuts).
Allergen information for Oreshki and Tiramisu depends on the specific filling, recipe and ingredients. Please contact us for product-specific allergen details. If you have a food allergy, please contact us before placing an order.

Storage Instructions

Brownies

Store according to the product-specific storage instructions provided with your order. Storage requirements may vary by flavor.

Oreshki

Store in an airtight container according to the filling's storage requirements and the instructions provided with your order.

Tiramisu

Keep refrigerated at 41°F (5°C) or below. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.

Safe Handling

Always follow the storage instructions provided on the product label. Do not consume products that have been stored improperly.

Allergen Cross-Contact

Our desserts are prepared in a home kitchen where different ingredients and food allergens may be handled.

Although we take care when preparing our products, we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Customers with severe food allergies should contact Marownie before ordering to discuss their specific allergy concerns.
