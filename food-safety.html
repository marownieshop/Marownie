Food Safety & Allergen Information

At Marownie, we prepare our desserts in a home kitchen using carefully selected ingredients. Your safety and satisfaction are important to us.

We believe in ingredient transparency. Please review the following information before placing your order.

Allergen Information

Allergen information varies by product, flavor, recipe and purchased ingredients. Please check the product-specific information below.

Brownies – Main Allergens

Based on the brownie ingredients currently identified, the main allergens are Eggs, Milk, Wheat and Soy (soy lecithin in the chocolate used).

Additional Allergens by Brownie Flavor

Classic Brownie: Contains the main brownie allergens listed above.

Cranberry Brownie: Contains the main brownie allergens listed above. Please check the cranberry product label for additional allergens.

Cream Cheese Brownie: Contains the main brownie allergens listed above, plus Milk from the cream cheese.

Pecan Chocolate Brownie: Contains the main brownie allergens listed above, plus Pecans (Tree Nuts).

Caramel & Peanut Brownie: Contains the main brownie allergens listed above, plus Peanuts.

Cranberry & Peanut Brownie: Contains the main brownie allergens listed above, plus Peanuts. Please check the cranberry product label for additional allergens.

Oreshki – Filled Cookies

Oreshki cookie shells contain Wheat (wheat flour), Eggs and Milk (butter and dairy ingredients).

Caramel-Filled Oreshki: Milk from the caramel or dairy filling ingredients.

Milk Chocolate Oreshki: Milk from milk chocolate and dairy ingredients. Soy may also be present depending on the chocolate label.

Peanut Butter Oreshki: Peanuts and Milk, in addition to the cookie shell allergens. Check the peanut butter label for any additional allergens.

Classic Tiramisu

Classic Tiramisu contains Eggs, Milk and Wheat, based on the ingredients listed for the recipe.

Brownie Tiramisu

Brownie Tiramisu contains Eggs, Milk and Wheat. Soy may also be present if the chocolate used in the brownie contains soy lecithin.

Exact allergen information may vary depending on the chocolate and other purchased ingredients.

Bird's Milk (Ptichye Moloko)

Based on the current soufflé recipe, Bird's Milk contains Eggs (egg whites) and Milk (mascarpone, butter and condensed milk). The chocolate coating may also contain Soy or additional Milk, depending on the chocolate label.

Ingredient labels can change. We check purchased ingredients and their packaging, but customers with food allergies should contact Marownie before ordering. We cannot guarantee that any product is free from a particular allergen.

Cross-contact: Wheat, Soy and Eggs.

Storage Instructions

Brownies

Store according to the product-specific storage instructions provided with your order. Storage requirements may vary by flavor.

Oreshki

Store in an airtight container according to the filling's storage requirements and the instructions provided with your order.

Tiramisu

Keep refrigerated at 41°F (5°C) or below. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.

Bird's Milk

Follow the product-specific storage instructions provided with your order.

Safe Handling

Always follow the storage instructions provided on the product label. Do not consume products that have been stored improperly.

THIS PRODUCT WAS PRODUCED IN A PRIVATE RESIDENCE THAT IS NOT SUBJECT TO GOVERNMENTAL LICENSING OR INSPECTION.

Questions About Allergens?

Please contact Marownie before placing your order if you have questions about ingredients, allergens, or storage instructions.
