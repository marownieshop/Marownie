Food Safety & Allergen Information

At Marownie, we prepare our homemade desserts with care and attention to ingredients. Please review the following information before placing an order, especially if you have food allergies or dietary restrictions.

Allergen Information

Our desserts may contain or come into contact with common allergens. Please review the ingredient information for each product carefully.

Ingredients and allergens used in our kitchen may include:

  • Wheat
  • Eggs
  • Milk and dairy products
  • Soy
  • Peanuts
  • Tree nuts, including almonds
  • Sesame
Important: Products are prepared in the same kitchen. We cannot guarantee that any product is completely free from allergens or cross-contact.

Product-Specific Allergen Information

Brownies

Brownies contain wheat, eggs and milk. Some varieties may also contain peanuts, tree nuts, soy or other ingredients depending on the recipe and the products used.

Oreshki

Our Oreshki contain wheat, eggs, milk and almonds (almond flour).

Peanut Butter & Strawberry Jam Oreshki also contain peanuts.

Additional allergens may be present depending on the chocolate and other packaged ingredients used.

Tiramisu

Our tiramisu contains eggs, milk and wheat. Additional allergens may depend on the ingredients used in each variety.

Please contact us before ordering if you have an allergy or need clarification about a specific dessert.

Storage Instructions

Brownies

Store in an airtight container at room temperature for a short period or refrigerate for longer storage. Allow refrigerated brownies to come closer to room temperature before serving.

Oreshki

Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Keep away from direct sunlight and heat.

Tiramisu

Keep refrigerated at all times. Do not leave tiramisu at room temperature for extended periods.

Safe Handling

Our desserts are prepared in small batches. We take care to handle ingredients and products responsibly and to keep our preparation area clean.

Please refrigerate products that require refrigeration promptly after pickup or delivery. Follow any storage instructions provided with your order.
