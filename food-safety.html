Food Safety & Allergen Information

At Marownie, we prepare our desserts in a home kitchen using carefully selected ingredients. Your safety and satisfaction are important to us.

Allergen Information

Our products may contain the following major food allergens, depending on the recipe and ingredients:

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Wheat
  • Soy
  • Peanuts
  • Tree Nuts
  • Sesame
Allergen information varies by product and flavor. Please review the ingredients and allergen information for each individual dessert before ordering. If you have a food allergy, please contact us before placing an order.

Storage Instructions

Brownies

Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Follow the product-specific storage instructions.

Oreshki

Store in an airtight container according to the filling's storage requirements.

Tiramisu

Keep refrigerated at 41°F (5°C) or below. Keep refrigerated until ready to serve.

Safe Handling

Always follow the storage instructions provided on the product label. Do not consume products that have been stored improperly.
